Mouse and touch

  • Keys and buttons play while held, several at once.
  • Knobs: drag up or down, the mouse wheel, or hover and press ↑ ↓.
  • To hold a button (GLO, FX, a step + a page button), right-click or long-press it; tap again to let go.

Getting around

  • PLAY starts demo 1. EDIT, ENV, FX and LFO open the 8-knob pages; press again for the lower row.
  • SELECT picks the machine on those pages. ALGORITHM picks the track.
  • SEQ: the steps on the white keys. SEL (SCL) turns KEYS off and on.
  • Hold GLO: the white keys mute the tracks. Hold HOME for the menu.

Computer keyboard

  • Lower keys: Z X C V B N M , . / and S D F H J L ; '
  • Upper keys: Q W E R T Y and 3 4 6.
  • Space PLAY, Enter REC, Esc HOME, ← OCT−, → OCT+.

How it works.

MNM-1 turns the FM-1 into six monophonic tracks, each holding one machine, each with the same pages of eight knobs. Everything below works the same in the simulator above and on the device.

Tracks and machines

Each of the six tracks holds one machine: a sound source or an FX machine. Pick the track with ALGORITHM; on an 8-knob page, SELECT changes its machine.

Synth machines

SWSAW
SuperWave saw: a detuned stack with subs.
SWPLS
SuperWave pulse with PWM and a second voice.
SID
A 6581-style oscillator: TRI, SAW, PULS, NOIS, with a second oscillator, sync and ring.
FM+
Two-operator FM with ratio, index, feedback and a modulator envelope.
DPRO
Wavetable scan and fold.
GND
Sine, triangle, noise or digital, with a pitch envelope: kicks, toms, tones.
VO-6
A formant voice: vowels, breath and resonance.

FX machines

THRU
Gain only: a second set of track effects for another track.
REVRB
Gated plate reverb: DEC DAMP GATE MIX HP LP.
CHRS
Stereo chorus.
DYNMX
Compressor and limiter.
RING
Ring modulator.
PHASR, FLANG
Phaser and flanger.

An FX machine makes no sound of its own: its input (EDIT, then the TRACK pages: IN) is the previous track (NEIB) or a bus (AB, CD, EF), and it passes audio once a trig opens its envelope. Each track's OUT picks its bus, or OFF.

The 8-knob pages

Every page has eight knobs in two rows of four, on the FM-1's KNOB 1-4. The row the knobs turn is lit; press the page's button again for the next row or page. The tab strip (SYN AMP FLT EFX LF1 LF2 LF3) shows where you are.

  • FILTER: a resonant high-pass at BASE into a low-pass at BASE + WDTH, each with its own Q. It follows the note: at BASE 0 the high-pass sits two octaves under it, 8 steps are an octave. An AD envelope adds BOFS to BASE and WOFS to WDTH.
  • EFFECTS: a peaking EQ (±36 dB), sample-rate reduction and a stereo tape delay on every track. DTIM is in 1/256 notes, synced to the tempo; DSND below 0 keeps the stereo image, above 0 ping-pongs; DFB above 64 grows into distortion.
  • LFOs: three a track, synced to the tempo. PAGE and DEST pick any knob of SYN, AMP, FLT or EFX, another LFO, or PTCH (vibrato up to 16 octaves). TRIG: FREE, TRIG, HOLD, ONE, HALF. Eleven waves. INTL interlaces the wave with silence.

Sequencer and parameter locks

SEQ puts the selected track's steps on the 16 white keys: tap a key to turn a step on or off. Press SEQ again for PATTERN (length, speed, swing, gate), MOTION, SONG, SWING MASK and TRANSPOSE. OCT− and OCT+ change the bank of 16 steps (up to 64).

  • Trig lanes: STEP knob 2 selects ALL, AMP, FILTER or LFO. Hold a step and turn SELECT to choose its trig type, including trigless and note-off.
  • Timing: SWING MASK sets a 50–80 amount and the delayed steps; 0 retains legacy swing. PATTERN adds 3/4X and 3/2X speeds. TRANSPOSE sets track amount, scale, key and pattern amount.
  • ARP: ARP MODE adds KEY, SID and ADD with five playback orders. ARP TRIG sets octave jump and retrigger switches; ARP RHYTHM edits a 16-step rhythm and note offsets.
  • Hold a step: KNOB 1 is its chance (PROB, and A/B conditions), KNOB 2 ratchets, KNOB 3 accent, KNOB 4 SLIDE.
  • Parameter locks: on STEP, hold a step and press EDIT, ENV, FX or LFO. The 8-knob page opens in LOCK mode, with the white keys still the steps. Hold any step and turn a knob: that value is locked on that step (shown inverted, with LOCK S5 on the screen). Hold OCT− and turn a knob to clear only that knob's lock. Tap OCT− to clear the held step's locks on release; SEQ leaves LOCK mode.
  • How locks play: a locked value lasts until the next trig, and an unlocked trig plays the kit's value. A lock on a step without a trig applies at that step.
  • Parameter slide: on a SLIDE step, every locked knob glides to its next lock on the track, arriving exactly there.
  • Live: press REC while playing and turn knobs: the moves are recorded as locks.
  • Notes: with KEYS on (the default; SEL toggles it), the keys play the selected track and OCT− / OCT+ set the octave. On STEP, stopped, REC starts step record.

More

  • HOME: a tap switches between TRACKS (levels, lengths, pans; SELECT sets the tempo) and HOME (the scope; SELECT on to the COMP sidechain compressor). Hold HOME for the menu.
  • GLO: a tap opens the global settings; held, the white keys mute the tracks.
  • FX held: PERFORM: the black keys play live master effects, the knobs are FILTER, CRUSH, THROW and DEPTH.
  • PRESETS (the knob): 330 factory presets, including 24 complete SOURCE sounds and 96 in ARCHIVE. Hold EDIT for the list; cancelling an audition restores the entire sound. Full calibrated USER saves require the expanded store.
  • Calibrated machines: the new synth and FX variants have separate sound VOL and mixer LEVEL, LFO target/phase controls, DESTROY and MOD FX pages: G-SIN, GPRC, SID2, FMST, FMDYN, SAW2, ENS, WAVE, VOX2, FMPAR, PUL2, CHO2, RING2, DYN2, NOISE, DKICK, DSNAR, DHAT and DCYMB. Original machines remain available. Controls marked n/a are unimplemented.
  • More FX: STRNG, PIPE, CHORD and MODAL machines; LOFI adds tape, crunch, hiss/dust and drive. REVERB TYPE adds STEREO. The panel layout stays the same.
  • SAVE: eight patterns, eight independent kits and 128 full user sounds after explicit library migration. Migration replaces sample slots 2 and 3, preserving slot 1; the legacy store remains available before migration. Simulator saves stay in your browser.

Roadmap.

0.10 is available now. Hardware stress testing is in progress; some heavy combinations exceed the audio deadline. What remains for 0.11 and 1.0 is listed below. The full list is in docs/ROADMAP.md.

Next: 0.11

  • TRIG POS: forward notes between tracks.
  • MULTI TRIG, keyboard split and a shared envelope.
  • Velocity and keytrack assignments, separate HP/LP tracking switches.

Then: 1.0

  • SONG rows with transpose, tempo, offset, length, mutes, loops and jumps.
  • The expanded pattern/kit/sound library is already implemented.

Maybe (CPU and memory)

  • 24 dB filter slopes (now 12 dB stages).
  • POLY mode; DPRO-BBOX; 6 MIDI tracks.
  • Longer full-quality delays.

Not on this hardware

  • External audio into FX machines (the FM-1 has no audio input) and six outputs.

Kept from Hortator

  • From Hortator: per-track length, PROB and A/B conditions, ratchets, accents, the slicer, PERFORM and the COMP compressor.

Put it on your FM-1.

From a web page, over the FM-1's own USB cable, in a few minutes.

Install

  1. Open the installer in Chrome or Edge

    On a computer: only they allow USB MIDI.

  2. Plug in the FM-1

    A USB data cable, straight into the computer.

  3. Press Install and wait

    Keep the cable in until Done. The FM-1 restarts with MNM-1.

    Open the installer

    Beta firmware, installed at your own risk: the author accepts no responsibility if your FM-1 is damaged or misbehaves.

    Back to the official firmware

    M-VAVE's updater, M-UPGRADE, puts it back: from m-vave.com/download, Downloads → PC Software → M-UPGRADE, with PC Firmware → FM-1 V15.

    Replug and press Install again: the FM-1 stays in update mode until a write has finished. If it no longer starts, recovery needs a Transporter.
